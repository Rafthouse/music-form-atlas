## 0. Purpose Of This Document

Atlas today contains **10 archetypes and 23 families**, almost all electronic. Opening it
feels like *"10 lessons with increasingly detailed notes."* The stated goal is the opposite: a
**large navigable map of musical form** — a *Wikipedia of compositional structures*, a *museum
of musical form*, an *atlas of music history*. The user should open it and feel the size of the
territory, not the smallness of a syllabus.

This document maps the **whole territory** so we can see how small the current coverage is, and
so every future entity has a known address before it is built.

It does **not** decide implementation order (see `ATLAS_EXPANSION_MASTERPLAN.md`). It maps.

## 1. The Classification Vocabulary (read this first)

Every entity in Atlas is exactly one of five kinds. The current project blurs these — the single
biggest ontological problem (see masterplan §3). Definitions used throughout this map:

| Kind | Definition | Test | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Archetype** | A genre-transcendent *strategy* for organising musical time and material. Survives translation across centuries and instruments. | "Could a composer in three unrelated genres/eras use this same organising idea?" | Theme & Variations, Fugue, Rondo |
| **Family** | A genre- and era-specific *implementation* of an archetype. | "Is this one community's recurring way of doing an archetype?" | Chicago House (implements Cyclic/Groove), Bebop head (implements Improvisation-Frame) |
| **Variant** | A named deviation *within* a family or archetype. | "Is this a tweak to one form, not a form of its own?" | 8-bar blues, AABA with tag, double-drop |
| **Historical Movement** | A time/place/scene that produced a cluster of works. *Context, not structure.* | "Is this a who/when/where, derivable from work dates + region?" | Second Viennese School, Detroit techno scene |
| **Production Tradition** | The philosophy + toolset + workflow of a community. *The "how."* | "Is this about gear, technique, and craft rather than form?" | Dub techno (spring reverb + tape echo + subtraction) |

**Altitude rule (proposed, currently violated):** Archetypes must sit at *comparable altitude*.
"Sonata-Allegro" (a universal developmental principle) and "12-Bar Blues" (one chord loop) are
**not** the same altitude and should not be peers. See §3 and the masterplan.

**Notation in tables below:**
`A` = should be an Archetype · `F` = Family · `V` = Variant · `HM` = Historical Movement ·
`PT` = Production Tradition · ✅ = already in Atlas · ⚠️ = partially present / mis-classified ·
❌ = missing.

## 2. The Proposed Backbone: 12 Macro-Archetypes (Form Principles)

Before the territory, the spine. After challenging the ontology (masterplan §3), the natural,
*altitude-consistent* set of organising principles is **12**, not 10. Everything in the territory
hangs off one of these. These are the "civilizations" a user should see on opening Atlas.

| # | Macro-Archetype | Core organising idea | Root problem served | Currently in Atlas? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | **Strophic / Sectional Song** | Repeat a unit; alternate a small set of sections (A, AB, AABA) | Predictability/Novelty | ⚠️ partial (Verse-Chorus only) |
| II | **Refrain-Return (Rondo / Ritornello)** | A home idea periodically returns between contrasts | Time + Predictability | ✅ (Rondo) |
| III | **Variation** | One idea transformed repeatedly (on melody, on bass, on harmony) | Predictability/Novelty | ⚠️ partial (Theme & Variations only; ground bass/passacaglia missing) |
| IV | **Developmental / Dialectical (Sonata)** | Material is split, transformed, recombined; key/tension drama | Time (directionality) | ✅ (Sonata-Allegro) |
| V | **Imitative / Contrapuntal (Fugue / Canon)** | Form generated by voices imitating one subject | Time + Contrast(texture) | ❌ **major gap** |
| VI | **Cyclic / Groove / Ostinato** | A repeating harmonic or rhythmic cycle is the form | Predictability | ⚠️ mis-modelled (12-bar is a *family* here; club loop, ground bass, vamp belong here) |
| VII | **Process / Gradual** | Form emerges from gradual transformation of minimal material | Time (directionality) | ✅ (Process-Minimal) |
| VIII | **Stasis / Drone** | Form from near-stillness and subtle internal change | Predictability (extreme) | ✅ (Drone) |
| IX | **Through-Composed / Narrative** | Continuous, non-repeating unfolding | Time | ✅ (Through-Composed) |
| X | **Improvisation-Frame** | A fixed frame hosts improvisation (head, raga, maqam, modal vamp) | Predictability/Novelty | ⚠️ partial (Head-Solo-Head = jazz only) |
| XI | **Energy-Arc / Build-Release** | Dramaturgy of tension accumulation and discharge | Time (directionality) | ⚠️ present but genre-bound (Club Arc = EDM only) |
| XII | **Moment / Mosaic / Collage** | Non-teleological succession of self-contained blocks; juxtaposition | Time (anti-directionality) | ❌ **major gap** |

> **Reading of the current 10:** Atlas covers **8 of 12** principles, but three of its slots
> (12-Bar Blues, Club Arc, Head-Solo-Head) are **single families pretending to be principles**,
> while two whole principles (Imitative/Contrapuntal, Moment/Mosaic) and large parts of two more
> (Strophic song forms, Variation-on-bass, non-Western improvisation) are **absent**.
> This is *why* it feels like 10 isolated examples rather than a map.

## 4. Scale Of The Gap (the headline number)

Counting distinct, addressable entities in the territory above:

| | Archetypes (macro) | Families | Variants | Movements |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mapped here** | 12 | ~110 | ~25 | ~12 |
| **Currently in Atlas** | 8 of 12 (3 mis-cast) | 23 (6 deep) | few | 0 |
| **Coverage** | ~40% (and unbalanced) | **~20%** | <10% | 0% |

Electronic music alone has ~30 families mapped; Atlas has ~13. The **entire pre-1900 Western
canon, all non-Western traditions, all sacred/operatic forms, and all of jazz beyond two forms
are effectively absent.** This is the breadth gap the user is reacting to.

## 5. What A "Map" Interface Implies (note for the masterplan)

A museum/atlas, not a syllabus, implies the data and UI must support:

1. **Browsable taxonomy** — Civilization → Macro-Archetype → Archetype → Family → Variant, with
   a visible count at every node ("Electronic · 31 families").
2. **Two cross-cutting axes** the same form can be filed under: *structural* (macro-archetype) and
   *historical* (civilization/era). The same form (e.g., Passacaglia) appears in Baroque **and**
   under Variation — a map has many roads to one city.
3. **Stub-first content** — a node may exist as a one-line stub (name, era, 1 canonical work) long
   before it is "deep-deployed." Breadth is allowed to be shallow. (This contradicts the current
   "everything must be deep" instinct — see masterplan.)
4. **Density over progressive disclosure** — show the territory at once; let depth be opt-in.

These are interface consequences, not part of this map. They are raised in
`ATLAS_EXPANSION_MASTERPLAN.md §6`.
