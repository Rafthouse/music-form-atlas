## 0. The Diagnosis (why this document exists)

The previous work (mine included) optimised **depth on a tiny footprint**: production traditions,
DAW translation, Club Arc, Dub Techno. Each addition was locally correct and globally wrong.

> **Atlas does not feel like a prototype because its archetypes are shallow.
> It feels like a prototype because it contains a tiny subset of music.**

The goal is **breadth first**: a large navigable map where a user feels the size of musical
territory on opening. Wikipedia of compositional structures. Museum of form. Not 10 lessons.

This plan accepts the user's explicit mandate to **reopen the architecture freeze for
examination** ("Challenge the existing ontology. Do not assume it is correct. Try to break it.").
The `ARCHITECTURE_FREEZE.md` constraints are treated here as *hypotheses to test*, not law.

## A) What Major Musical Territories Are Missing?

Full inventory is in `ATLAS_TERRITORY_MAP.md`. Summary verdict per requested territory:

| Territory | Status in Atlas | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| **Classical (era)** | Sonata, Rondo, Theme&Var present. Minuet, Scherzo, Sonata-Rondo, Concerto, the symphony-as-cycle missing. | High |
| **Medieval** | Absent entirely (chant, organum, isorhythm, formes fixes, estampie) | **Critical — 0%** |
| **Renaissance** | Absent (Mass, motet, madrigal, ricercar, ground, dance pairs) | **Critical — 0%** |
| **Baroque** | Absent (Fugue, canon, ritornello, passacaglia, chaconne, binary, suite, da capo aria) | **Critical — 0%, contains the single most important missing archetype: FUGUE** |
| **Romantic** | Absent (Lied, character piece, symphonic poem, song cycle, thematic transformation) | **Critical — 0%** |
| **Modernist** | Process/minimal present. Moment form, arch form, open/aleatoric, serial, spectral, sound-mass, collage missing. | High |
| **Jazz** | 2 of ~11 (blues, bebop head). Rhythm changes, AABA, modal, big band, free jazz absent. | High |
| **Folk / World** | Absent (strophic ballad, dance tune, raga, maqam, gamelan, clave, drone-folk). Non-Western ≈ 0. | **Critical — and ironic given the user plays Irish bouzouki** |
| **Sacred** | Absent (Mass, chorale, gospel, qawwali, chant) | **Critical — 0%** |
| **Ambient** | Present as a shallow Drone family | Medium |
| **Electronic** | Strongest area: ~13 of ~30 families, 6 deep | Medium (breadth still thin) |
| **Experimental** | Absent (musique concrète, plunderphonics, noise, generative beyond a stub) | High |

**Bottom line:** Of the twelve civilizations, **six are at literal zero** (Medieval, Renaissance,
Baroque, Romantic, Folk/World, Sacred). The map is ~80% empty, and the empty 80% includes the
entire historical canon and the entire non-Western world.

## B) Which Missing Entities Should Be Archetypes / Families / Variants / Movements?

Classification rules are defined in `ATLAS_TERRITORY_MAP.md §1`. The high-value reclassifications
and additions:

### Should be ARCHETYPES (new, genre-transcendent principles)
| Entity | Why an archetype (not a family) |
|---|---|
| **Fugue / Imitative-Contrapuntal** | A universal strategy: form generated by voices imitating one subject. Spans Renaissance motet → Bach → Bartók → jazz (Mingus) → electronic counterpoint. Not bound to one genre. **Highest-priority new archetype.** |
| **Ground-Bass Variation (Passacaglia/Chaconne/Ostinato)** | Variation over a *repeating bass/harmonic cycle*. Distinct from Theme&Variations (varies a melody). The deepest bridge classical↔electronic (ground bass = the loop). |
| **Ritornello / Refrain-Return** | Already half-present as Rondo; ritornello (tutti/solo opposition) generalises Rondo into a full Refrain-Return principle. |
| **Moment Form / Mosaic / Collage** | Non-teleological juxtaposition of self-contained blocks. Stockhausen → Ives → Berio → plunderphonics → DJ mix → breakcore. A genuinely different organisation of time. **Second-priority new archetype.** |
| **Improvisation-Frame (generalised)** | Promote Head-Solo-Head into a principle that also hosts Raga, Maqam/Taqsim, Modal vamp, Gospel drive. |
| **Energy-Arc / Build-Release (generalised)** | Promote Club Arc into a principle that also hosts post-rock crescendo, bel canto cabaletta, big-band shout chorus, bolero, EDM. |
| **Multi-Movement Cycle ("form of forms")** | Suite, symphony, song cycle, concept album, DJ set, opera. A higher-altitude meta-archetype — *optional*, see risk note in §3. |
| **Strophic / AABA (song forms)** | Either as families under a "Sectional Song" archetype, or AABA promoted given its dominance in jazz/standards. |

### Should be FAMILIES (genre implementations of an archetype)
Almost everything in `ATLAS_TERRITORY_MAP.md §3` marked `F`: e.g. Madrigal (→ Through-Composed),
Da capo aria (→ Refrain-Return), Lied (→ Strophic), Rhythm changes (→ Cyclic), Modal jazz
(→ Cyclic/Improv), all electronic subgenres, all dance tunes, gospel, qawwali, etc.

### Should be VARIANTS (deviations within a form)
8-bar/16-bar blues, contrafact, scherzo (of minuet), sonatina, AABA-with-tag, double-drop,
modified-strophic, French overture, mouth music, anthem arc.

### Should be HISTORICAL MOVEMENTS (context, not structure — a secondary layer)
Ars Nova, Second Viennese School, Detroit/Chicago/Berlin scenes, hard bop, postminimalism,
Hyperdub/DMZ, fusion. These hang off works (derivable from date + place), exactly as the brief
already concluded. **Do not promote movements to primary navigation; attach them to families.**

### Should be PRODUCTION TRADITIONS (the "how")
Dub-techno spring-reverb craft, bebop recording, Baroque continuo realisation, gamelan tuning,
musique concrète tape splicing. These are the deep layer — *but breadth comes first.*

## C) Is The Current Set Of 10 Archetypes Sufficient? — Breaking The Ontology

**No.** Here is the adversarial analysis the user asked for.

### C.1 The fatal flaw: inconsistent altitude
The 10 archetypes are not peers. They live at three different altitudes:

| Altitude | Examples in current set | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| **Universal principle** | Sonata-Allegro, Rondo, Theme & Variations, Through-Composed | Correct altitude |
| **Genre-bound dramaturgy** | Club Arc (EDM), Head-Solo-Head (jazz) | Too low — these are *families* of bigger principles |
| **Single template** | 12-Bar Blues (one chord loop) | Far too low — this is a *variant/family*, not a principle |

A map with "Sonata" and "12-Bar Blues" as siblings is like an atlas with "Asia" and "the corner
shop on my street" at the same zoom level. This is the core reason it reads as "isolated
examples."

### C.2 Missing archetypes (the ontology has holes)
- **Imitative/Contrapuntal (Fugue)** — an entire organising principle absent. Indefensible for a
  map of musical form.
- **Moment/Mosaic/Collage** — the principle behind a century of modernism *and* of DJ/mashup/
  breakcore culture. Absent.
- **Ground-Bass Variation** — the classical↔electronic bridge. Absent (and Theme&Variations does
  not cover it: melody-variation ≠ bass-variation).
- **Stasis/Drone vs Process** — present, good, but their kinship (the "non-teleological" family)
  is not modelled, so they look arbitrary.

### C.3 Redundancy / things that should merge or split
- **Split:** "Verse-Chorus" silently swallows three distinct song strategies — **Strophic**,
  **AABA/32-bar**, **Verse-Chorus**. They make different predictability/novelty trade-offs and
  should be separate families (at minimum) under one "Sectional Song" archetype.
- **Demote:** **12-Bar Blues → family** of a new **Cyclic/Groove/Ostinato** archetype (peers:
  rhythm changes, ground bass, modal vamp, club loop, gamelan cycle, clave montuno). Its prized
  pedagogical point — "form from harmony, not theme" — *is precisely the Cyclic archetype's
  thesis*. Keeping 12-bar as a top-level archetype hides that thesis.
- **Generalise (don't delete):** **Club Arc → family** of **Energy-Arc/Build-Release**;
  **Head-Solo-Head → family** of **Improvisation-Frame**. Keep them as the flagship families
  (they're excellent), but stop pretending they are the principle.

### C.4 What stays exactly as-is
Sonata-Allegro, Rondo, Theme & Variations, Through-Composed, Process-Minimal, Drone — all are
genuine, correctly-altituded archetypes. Keep.

### C.5 The verdict: how many archetypes?
Not 10. The altitude-consistent backbone is **12 macro-archetypes** (see TERRITORY_MAP §2). The
current 10 collapse to **6 valid archetypes + 3 mis-cast families + 1 mis-cast variant**, and the
map needs **~6 new principles** (Fugue, Ground-Bass Variation, Ritornello/Refrain-Return,
Moment/Mosaic, generalised Improvisation-Frame, generalised Energy-Arc) plus an optional 13th
meta-archetype (Multi-Movement Cycle).

> **Recommended target: 12 macro-archetypes, ~110 families, staged.** Not 30 flat archetypes
> (that would re-create the altitude problem one level up). Depth of *hierarchy*, not a long flat
> list, is what makes it a navigable map.

### C.6 Tension with the FREEZE — resolved honestly
`ARCHITECTURE_FREEZE.md` says "do not add archetypes / do not redesign architecture." The brief
and the user now explicitly *want* this challenged. Reconciliation:

- The **Two Root Problems** and **4 Core Concepts** stay frozen. They survive — in fact the
  12 macro-archetypes map cleanly onto the two root problems (see TERRITORY_MAP §2). **The
  foundation is vindicated, not broken.**
- What changes is the *middle layer*: a consistent **Macro-Archetype → Archetype → Family →
  Variant** hierarchy replaces the flat 10. This is an *extension* of the frozen model, not a
  return to Form Dimensions. No sliders, no continuous space — still discrete, concrete,
  nameable forms. The freeze's actual intent (no dimensions, no templates, concrete forms) is
  preserved.

## D) The Complete Historical Map

The full map is `ATLAS_TERRITORY_MAP.md §3` (12 civilizations × forms × classification). It is
built on **two cross-cutting axes**, because a real atlas has many roads to one city:

```
AXIS 1 — STRUCTURAL (the 12 macro-archetypes)   ← "how is time organised?"
AXIS 2 — HISTORICAL (12 civilizations / eras)    ← "when/where did it live?"

A single form occupies a cell in BOTH grids.
  e.g. PASSACAGLIA  = Axis1: III Variation (Ground-Bass)   Axis2: Baroque
       It is reachable from "Variation" AND from "Baroque".
```

This dual-axis is the difference between a **museum** (walk by gallery = era, or by theme =
principle) and a **syllabus** (one linear path). Implementing both axes over the same dataset is
the central data-model requirement (see §6).

**Lineages worth drawing as explicit "roads" on the map** (narrative spines that make it feel
alive):
1. Ground bass → Passacaglia/Chaconne → blues/rhythm-changes cycle → house/techno loop → ground-bass renaissance (electronic)
2. Renaissance imitation → Ricercar → Fugue → fugal jazz/film → contrapuntal electronics
3. Binary dance → Rounded binary → Sonata → cyclic form → symphonic poem → film leitmotif
4. Formes fixes (rondeau) → Rondo → Sonata-Rondo → pop verse-chorus return logic
5. Chant drone → folk drone (bagpipe/tanpura) → La Monte Young → Eno → ambient → dub techno
6. Call-and-response (Africa) → work song → blues → gospel → soul → hip-hop hook
7. Moment form → collage (Ives/Berio) → plunderphonics → mashup → DJ set → breakcore

## E) Localization Audit (summary — full plan in LOCALIZATION_REBUILD_PLAN.md)

Audited directly against `app.js`. Headline findings (evidence in the dedicated doc):

- **Content is 100% Ukrainian-only.** No bilingual content fields exist; only ~66 UI-label keys
  are translated. Switching to EN yields a **mixed-language screen** (English labels, Ukrainian
  body).
- **Hardcoded strings bypass i18n:** `Відмінні ознаки` (UK), `Філософія:` (UK),
  **`Listening Pathway` (English literal inside a UK UI)**, `Пов'язані сімейства:` (UK).
- **Key parity broken:** `en` has `vsLabel`, `uk` does not → UK mode renders the raw key
  `"vsLabel"`.
- **`t()` fails loud:** on a missing key it returns the *key name* to the user.
- **Classic Atlas has zero i18n** (no `state.lang`, no `t()`); ~70 forms, single-language hardcode,
  cannot switch at all.
- **`<html lang="uk">` hardcoded** in both apps; never reflects the chosen language.

Verdict: localisation is **not production-acceptable**. Full rebuild plan: see
`LOCALIZATION_REBUILD_PLAN.md`.

## 6. Interface & Data-Model Consequences (what "map not syllabus" requires)

Breadth changes the architecture's needs. These are design requirements, not yet decisions:

1. **Hierarchical, browsable taxonomy** with live counts at every node
   (`Electronic · 31 families`, `Baroque · 14 forms`). The landing screen should show the *whole*
   taxonomy collapsed — the user should *see* the scale immediately.
2. **Dual-axis navigation** (structural ↔ historical) over one dataset (§D).
3. **Stub-first content model.** A node may be `{name, era, oneLineDef, 1 canonical work}` and
   nothing more. **Breadth is allowed to be shallow.** This explicitly reverses the prior
   "everything must be deep-deployed" instinct — that instinct is what kept the map tiny.
4. **Density over progressive disclosure** for the primary audience (composers/teachers): show
   territory at once; make depth opt-in. (Aligns with brief's UI-mistake #11.)
5. **Data scale forces a file split.** ~110 families cannot live in one 600 KB `app.js`. Move
   content to data files (e.g. `data/<civilization>.js` or `data/<macroArchetype>.js`), keep
   render logic separate. (Brief Refactor #6 — now *required*, not optional.)
6. **Search/filter becomes mandatory** at this scale (brief Expand #5).

> Note: items 3–6 are the parts of the current architecture that actively *prevent* breadth. The
> "deep-deploy everything" + "single file" + "progressive disclosure" combination is optimised
> for a 10-lesson course, not a museum.

## 7. Recommended Phased Plan (breadth-first; for a LATER implementation pass)

No implementation now. This is the proposed order once architecture is agreed.

| Phase | Goal | Content target | Why this order |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Architecture** | Agree the 12 macro-archetype hierarchy + dual axis + stub model + file split. *No content.* | — | Truth before building |
| **1. Skeleton breadth** | Stub **every** form in the territory map (name, era, 1-line def, 1 canonical work, macro-archetype tag). | ~110 families as stubs | Instantly turns 10 examples into a full map; the "overwhelming" effect appears here |
| **2. Fill the empty civilizations** | Bring the six zero-coverage eras to baseline depth (3–5 lines each). | Medieval, Renaissance, Baroque, Romantic, Folk/World, Sacred | Removes the "Western-electronic-only" feel |
| **3. New archetypes** | Author the missing principles: Fugue, Ground-Bass Variation, Moment/Mosaic, generalised Improv-Frame & Energy-Arc. | 5–6 archetypes | Fixes the ontology holes |
| **4. Reclassify** | Demote 12-Bar Blues → family; generalise Club Arc & Head-Solo-Head; split song forms. *(Non-destructive: keep all existing content, re-parent it.)* | — | Fixes altitude |
| **5. Selective depth** | Deep-deploy ONE family per civilization as an exemplar (incl. one non-electronic, per brief). | ~12 deep families | Proves depth scales beyond electronic |
| **6. Localization rebuild** | Per `LOCALIZATION_REBUILD_PLAN.md`. | — | Best done before content explodes further |

**Sequencing principle:** *breadth (Phases 1–2) before depth (Phase 5).* The exact inverse of the
work done so far. Depth on a near-empty map is premature optimisation.

## 8. Risks & Honest Caveats

- **Scope explosion.** ~110 families is a multi-month content effort even at stub depth. Phase 1
  must be ruthlessly shallow or it stalls.
- **The Multi-Movement-Cycle meta-archetype** (suite/symphony/album/DJ-set) is a different
  *altitude* again (a form of forms). Tempting but risky — recommend deferring it; model it as a
  cross-link, not a 13th archetype, until the 12 are stable.
- **Non-Western forms need real expertise.** Raga/maqam/gamelan must not be flattened into Western
  terms (the brief's "universal form language" failure, lesson #15). Stub them honestly or consult
  sources; do not fake depth.
- **The freeze.** Reclassification (Phase 4) edits the sacred middle layer. It is *non-destructive*
  (re-parenting, not deleting) but must be explicitly blessed by the user before any code.
- **This document is opinionated by request.** The 12-archetype backbone is a strong proposal, not
  the only possible cut. The user should contest any boundary that doesn't match their teaching
  experience — especially the demotion of 12-Bar Blues, which is pedagogically beloved.

## 9. The One Decision That Unblocks Everything

Before any further content or code, one architectural choice must be made by the user:

> **Adopt the Macro-Archetype hierarchy (12 principles) + dual-axis (structural × historical) +
> stub-first model?**  Yes / No / Modified.

Everything else (territory map, localisation, phases) is execution under that decision. Until it
is made, adding more content — in any genre, at any depth — only grows the prototype, not the map.
