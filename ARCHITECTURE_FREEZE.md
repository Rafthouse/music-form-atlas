# ARCHITECTURE_FREEZE.md — Music Form Atlas

## Core Architecture (FROZEN)

Atlas = **Core Concepts** + **Form Archetypes**

This architecture is frozen. No replacement with Form Dimensions, sliders, profiles, or multi-layer ontology.

### What Atlas IS

A pedagogical tool that teaches musical form through **compositional decisions**. A student sees a concrete form, opens it, analyzes its sections, and learns how to build it.

### What Atlas is NOT

- Not an encyclopedia of music history
- Not a classification system
- Not a theoretical ontology
- Not a dimensional slider tool

## Form Archetypes (5, FROZEN — can add more)

### 1. Verse-Chorus (Pop)
- **id:** verse-chorus
- **Generated by:** segmentation, repetition, contrast
- **Genre:** pop / rock
- **Difficulty:** easy
- **Sections:** Intro, Verse 1, Pre-chorus, Chorus, Verse 2, Bridge, Final chorus

### 2. Club Arc (Electronic)
- **id:** club-arc
- **Generated by:** segmentation, contrast, directionality
- **Genre:** house / techno
- **Difficulty:** easy
- **Sections:** Intro, Groove, Build, Drop, Breakdown, Rebuild, Second drop, Outro

### 3. Rondo (Classical)
- **id:** rondo
- **Generated by:** segmentation, repetition, contrast
- **Genre:** classical
- **Difficulty:** medium
- **Sections:** A (refrain), B episode, A return, C episode, A final

### 4. 12-Bar Blues (NEW in v1.5)
- **id:** 12-bar-blues
- **Generated by:** segmentation, repetition
- **Genre:** blues / rock / jazz
- **Difficulty:** easy
- **Sections:** I (Tonic) 4 bars, IV (Subdominant) 2 bars, I (Tonic return) 2 bars, V (Dominant) 2 bars, IV (Cadence) 1 bar, I (Resolution) 1 bar
- **Pedagogical value:** Єдина форма, що базується на ГАРМОНІЇ, а не на темах. Навчає: форма може бути гармонійним циклом.

### 5. Process-Based Minimal (Contemporary)
- **id:** process-minimal
- **Generated by:** segmentation, repetition, directionality
- **Genre:** minimal / contemporary
- **Difficulty:** hard
- **Sections:** Cell, Layering, Process shift, Accumulation, Dissolution

## Implemented Content Layers (per Form Archetype, FROZEN)

Each form has these expandable/collapsible sections (progressive disclosure):

| # | Layer | Icon | Data field |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Section Knowledge Cards | 📖 | `sectionKnowledge[]` |
| 2 | Failure Analysis | 💥 | `failureAnalysis[]` |
| 3 | Variant Library | 🔀 | `variants[]` |
| 4 | Canonical Tracks | 🎵 | `canonicalTracks[]` |
| 5 | Self-Diagnosis | 🔍 | `diagnosis[]` |
| 6 | Genre Transfer | 🌍 | `genreTransfer[]` |
| 7 | DAW Exercise (NEW v1.5) | ⛏ | `dawExercise[]` |
| 8 | Listening Exercise (NEW v1.5) | 🎵 | `listeningExercise[]` |
| 9 | Comparisons (NEW v1.5) | 🔍 | `comparisons[]` |

### Section Knowledge Cards
Per section: purpose, whyItWorks, commonMistake, whenToSkip, composerDecision, realExamples[]

### Failure Analysis
Per section: section name, removal description, effects[]

### Variants
Per variant: name, used (genre/context), why, tradeoffs

### Canonical Tracks
Per track: title, artist, year, genre (NEW v1.4), formNotes, sections[{name, time}], analysis[{segmentation, repetition, contrast, directionality}] (NEW v1.4)

### Diagnosis
Array of self-evaluation questions (strings)

### Genre Transfer
Per genre: genre name, description, adaptation

### DAW Exercise (NEW v1.5)
Array of step-by-step DAW tasks (strings)

### Listening Exercise (NEW v1.5)
Array of directed listening prompts (strings)

### Comparisons (NEW v1.5)
Array of {with, difference} objects comparing to other archetypes
